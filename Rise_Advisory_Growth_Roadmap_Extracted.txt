1. Brand positioning

What Rise Advisory is

A managed platform for fractional executives, senior advisors, coaches and specialist delivery partners. It diagnoses the company’s growth constraint, assigns the right expert or cross-functional pod, and remains accountable for the engagement rhythm and agreed outputs.

What it is not

Not a staffing agency that only introduces candidates.

Not a marketplace where clients must manage disconnected freelancers.

Not a general agency claiming expertise in every field.

Not a motivational coaching business without operational delivery.

Not a legal, investment-banking, insurance-broking or regulated financial-services provider unless appropriately licensed partners deliver that scope.

Specialist network layer

Photography, legal coordination, insurance, loans, taxation, design, learning, wellness and other specialists should be presented as “Specialist Network” or “Partner Services,” not mixed into the hero message. Each regulated service requires credential checks, disclosures and a clear statement of who is responsible for advice.

Spirituality and workplace wellbeing

Position this as optional “Founder & Team Performance” support. Use evidence-oriented language such as mindfulness, reflection, stress management, values alignment and facilitated wellbeing sessions. Avoid presenting spirituality as the reason a company will financially grow.

Commercial rules

Three-month minimum for most retainers; one-month pilot only for a tightly defined outcome.

Collect setup/diagnostic fees upfront and monthly retainers in advance.

Separate third-party spend, travel, media, tools and statutory fees.

Use change requests for work outside the agreed outcome and capacity.

Pay experts through a transparent fixed share/day rate; protect gross margin through a management and delivery layer.

Target blended gross margin of 35%–50% after expert payouts; track it per engagement.

Recommended service guarantee

The Rise Execution Guarantee
If Rise Advisory misses an agreed deliverable or governance milestone for reasons within its control, it will complete that item without an additional professional fee or extend the relevant delivery period, subject to client dependencies defined in the Statement of Work.

Never guarantee revenue, profitability, funding, loan approval, IPO approval, market valuation, Google rankings or a fixed transformation result. Claims should be truthful and substantiated; ASCI’s code emphasizes honest, non-misleading advertising.

19. Final recommendations

Lead with Fractional C-Suite and business growth—not “every consultancy service.”

Sell a diagnostic first; use it to match the right role and engagement.

Launch with five service pillars and four priority CXO pages; expand only after proof.

Make execution governance the differentiator: one lead, one dashboard, one cadence.

Replace blanket guarantees with a specific, contract-backed execution guarantee.

Keep regulated services partner-led and properly disclosed.

Use local founder networks and referral partners for early pipeline while SEO compounds.

Build proof from day one: baseline, milestones, accepted outcomes and client permission.

Treat team wellbeing/spirituality as an optional performance service, not the central growth promise.

Review positioning, margins, lead quality and client outcomes every quarter.
